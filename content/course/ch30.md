# 30. Belief Change: Worked Examples

<p class='subtitle'>Two worked examples</p>

<p>Watch for the structure (A → B, C → D), the speed, the hand-pointing for location, and how the practitioner repeats each submodality in command tonality. When the practitioner does it live he adds a little, but the structure is the same every time.</p>

## Worked example 1

<p><b>Set-up:</b> commitment 10/10; ecology question answered "yes". <b>Secondary gain</b> here.</p>

<div class='table-wrap'><table><thead><tr><th></th><th>A: "I'm broken and a crap person"</th><th>B: "I am a 7-year-old child"</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Picture</td><td>She sees the words. Colour, near (touching distance), <b>Associated</b>, still</td><td>Colour, quite near but <b>Behind her</b>, still</td></tr><tr><td>Sounds</td><td>Her own voice, internal, soft</td><td>Children's laughter, soft but firm</td></tr><tr><td>Feelings</td><td>Painful, ashamed, worthless; dark olive green; 9/10</td><td>Playfulness, laughter, "a bit ridiculous, but seven was cool"; bright yellow</td></tr><tr><td>Self-talk</td><td>"That's right, I need to accept it's true"</td><td>"What the heck are you trying to convince yourself of?"</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

<p><b>Mapping:</b> the practitioner had her watch the image move <b>All the way behind her</b> and disappear, as a still image, with children's laughter, playfulness, yellow washing over her and the self-talk "What was I doing that for in the first place?" Tupperware lock. Her response: <b>"What have I been doing?"</b></p>

<p><b>New belief (D):</b> "<b>I can change and make a real, tangible difference.</b>" (the practitioner quotes Mother Teresa: "be a pencil in the hand of God".)</p>

<div class='table-wrap'><table><thead><tr><th></th><th>C: "The sun will rise tomorrow"</th><th>D installed</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Picture</td><td>Colour, <b>Far away on the horizon</b>, moving, associated, panoramic</td><td>Bright and alive, sent all the way out to the horizon, associated, a panoramic movie of her whole life making a difference</td></tr><tr><td>Sounds</td><td>Beach sounds, birds at dawn, soft</td><td>Beach, birds</td></tr><tr><td>Feelings</td><td>Certainty, in her head, 6/10, shaded deep orange</td><td>Certainty built from 6 to 10; orange washing over her</td></tr><tr><td>Self-talk</td><td>"It's good, and I hope the day's different from this one"</td><td>"...and that I get to continue my mission to change the world"</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Worked example 2

<p>Done online in front of 145 people in 12 countries.</p>

### Finding the real belief (drilling down)

<div class='table-wrap'><table><thead><tr><th>The practitioner asks</th><th>The client says</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>What's your limiting belief?</td><td>"I'm not good enough and I'll fail in front of everybody."</td></tr><tr><td>What's the problem with you failing?</td><td>"People will see I'm not able to do what I wanted... maybe imposter syndrome."</td></tr><tr><td>And if they see that, what would it validate about yourself that you believe?</td><td>"<b>That I'm not good enough.</b>"</td></tr><tr><td>What has it cost you (wellness, income, love, lifestyle, direction)?</td><td>She lost nine stone with Weight Watchers and put it back on, then avoided friends and family out of shame. A program helping people move from corporate jobs to entrepreneurship has been on hold for a year; "there's always something".</td></tr><tr><td>What would it mean to be rid of it? / Commitment 0–10?</td><td>"Everything." / "100."</td></tr><tr><td>Ecology question</td><td>"Yes."</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

### Part 1: remove

<div class='table-wrap'><table><thead><tr><th></th><th>A: "I'm not good enough"</th><th>B: "I am a 7-year-old child"</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Picture</td><td>Colour, <b>Near (30 cm in front of her face)</b>, dissociated, framed, still, bright</td><td>Colour, <b>Far</b>, dissociated, located <b>In her heart</b>, fast panoramic movie; happiness, being cheeky</td></tr><tr><td>Sounds</td><td>Social media notifications on her phone; loud, external</td><td>Family all around her; loud but good, external</td></tr><tr><td>Feelings</td><td>Embarrassment, fear; 8/10; yellow</td><td>Confidence, complete happiness; teal (bluey-green); "off the scale"</td></tr><tr><td>Self-talk</td><td>"I'll fail."</td><td>"I don't really believe I'm seven." How do you know? "Because I have doubts."</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

<ul><li><b>Break state:</b> "Want some popcorn? I thought I could smell popcorn."</li><li><b>Mapping:</b> make it a fast, panoramic movie; far away; step out and watch it like an old movie; happiness and cheekiness; move it from in front of her face <b>Into her heart</b>; loud, good family sounds; confidence; teal "from the tips of your toes to the longest hair on your head"; happiness to 10, then infinite; "I don't really believe it... because of course I've got some doubts. Same way I don't believe I'm seven." Tupperware lock, twice.</li><li><b>Test:</b> "What comes up for you now?" "<b>Just family and teal.</b>" Still a problem? "<b>I don't believe that.</b>" How do you know? "Because I feel confident in myself and my ability" (in her heart).</li></ul>

### Part 2: install

<div class='table-wrap'><table><thead><tr><th></th><th>C: "The sun will rise tomorrow"</th><th>D: "I can achieve anything"</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Picture</td><td>Colour, far but in front (on the horizon), associated, panoramic, slow movie, orangey warmth</td><td>Coloured, bright and alive; far away on the horizon; associated; panoramic slow movie filling her whole future; orange</td></tr><tr><td>Sounds</td><td>Birds singing; her own slow, deep breaths; loud; internal and external</td><td>Birds chirping loudly; her heartbeat; slow deep breaths</td></tr><tr><td>Feelings</td><td>Love and warmth, in her heart, "11, 12" out of 10; yellow</td><td>Love and warmth; yellow; built 8 → 10 → 12</td></tr><tr><td>Self-talk</td><td>"I'm blessed."</td><td>"I'm blessed" and "I can achieve anything."</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

<ul><li>The practitioner skipped eliciting D's submodalities: it's "just a thought" with no blueprint yet, so <b>"this space is intentionally left blank"</b>; Part 2 is half the work.</li><li>Final touches: make it "full HD", double it, lock it, and "store it in the place you reserve for all such learnings".</li><li><b>Future pace:</b> "Jump into some time in the future where in the past you'd have been held back... how are you acting differently?" "Lots of energy, very confident, quite certain about myself and my ability." How do you know? "I feel it," in her heart; yellowy.</li></ul>

<div class='box key'><div class='box-title'>Key Takeaway</div><p>Notice how often the 7-year-old belief sits <b>Far away, behind you or out of view</b>, and the sun sits <b>Far away on the horizon</b>. Moving the limiting belief to the old belief's <b>Location</b> is usually what does the work.</p>

<p>The practitioner used both demos to show <b>Outcome 1</b> (a visible lightness and shift in the face). Watch for the physiology (Chapter 9).</p>
